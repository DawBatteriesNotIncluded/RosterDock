# How Cirrus writes to customers

Everything a customer reads from Cirrus follows this, whoever or whatever wrote it.

## Principles

1. **Say what happened, in the first sentence.** "Your broadband was down on Saturday evening", not "some
   customers may have experienced intermittent issues".
2. **Own it.** If it was our fault, say so plainly, once. Never "we apologise for any inconvenience caused".
3. **Be specific.** Times, places and what to do next. If we do not know yet, say when we will.
4. **Promise only what the policy gives.** Money, credits and dates come from `policies/`, word for word.
   If the policy gives nothing automatic, say how to ask and what we will consider.
5. **Plain words.** No "outage event", "service degradation" or "valued customer". Broadband, Wi-Fi, down, back.
6. **Short.** A text message is 160 characters or fewer, with no link shorteners. An email says what
   happened, what we did, what it means for you and how to reach us, in that order, in under 180 words.

## Words

| Write             | Not                        |
| ----------------- | -------------------------- |
| down, not working | outage event, degradation  |
| we got it wrong   | an issue occurred          |
| back at 22:55     | has now been resolved      |
| your Hub          | the CPE, the router device |
| text              | SMS (to customers)         |

## Customers who rely on their line

Anything written for priority customers (see `policies/priority-customers.md`) leads with the phone number
of the priority team and never asks them to use the app or chat first.

## Stream editorial

Picks are one or two sentences each, say why now, and name the age rating. Never recommend a title that
leaves Stream within 14 days without saying so.
