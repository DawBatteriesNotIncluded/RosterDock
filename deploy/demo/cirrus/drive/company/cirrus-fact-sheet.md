# Cirrus fact sheet

Cirrus is a fictional company made for a RosterDock demo. This sheet is the only source of product and price
claims: if it is not here, a teammate does not say it.

## The company

- Cirrus provides full-fibre broadband, TV over broadband and mobile to 2.4 million UK households.
- Customer care: phone 8am to 9pm every day; chat and the Cirrus app at any hour, where the care assistant
  **Ask Cirrus** answers first and hands over to a person when it should (see `policies/assistant-guardrails.md`).
- Service status: `status.cirrus.example`, updated by the network operations centre. The target is an entry
  within 15 minutes of a fault affecting more than 1,000 customers.
- Regions: North West, London, Scotland, Midlands, South West, Yorkshire, Wales.

## Broadband

| Plan      | Average download | Price a month | Contract  |
| --------- | ---------------- | ------------- | --------- |
| Fibre 150 | 150 Mbps         | £29           | 18 months |
| Fibre 500 | 500 Mbps         | £38           | 18 months |
| Fibre 900 | 900 Mbps         | £49           | 18 months |

- Every plan comes with the Cirrus Hub (Hub 3 since 2025; Hub 2 before). Hub lights: white is working,
  amber flashing is connecting or updating, red is no connection.
- Hub software updates install overnight between 2am and 5am.
- Changing plan mid-contract is free; the new price starts from the next bill.

## Cirrus Stream

- TV over broadband through the Stream box or the Stream app; it needs a working broadband connection.
- Stream: £15 a month. Packs: Cinema £10, Sport £22, Kids £5 a month, each on a 30-day rolling term.
- Every title has subtitles; audio description is on most and shown on the title's page.
- A title leaves Stream on its licence end date and is shown as "Leaving soon" for its last 14 days.

## Cirrus Mobile

- SIM-only plans from £10 a month, on a 30-day rolling term; eSIM is free on request.
- Roaming in the EU costs £2 a day, charged only on days the phone is used abroad.

## What Cirrus does not offer

- No landline-only service, no business broadband, no static IP addresses.
- No compensation or credit beyond `policies/compensation-policy.md`.
