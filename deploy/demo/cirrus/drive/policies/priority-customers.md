# Priority customers

Fictional, for the Cirrus demo.

A priority customer is anyone who has told us they rely on their line: a telecare or fall alarm, a medical
monitor, a relay service for deaf customers, or no other way to call for help. The account carries the flag;
a contact from a flagged account is marked `priority_customer` in the contacts export.

## What we promise

1. A priority customer who reports no service gets a **callback from a person within 2 hours**, at any hour.
2. If the fault will last longer than 4 hours, we offer a mobile broadband device, delivered the same day.
3. They reach the priority team directly on **0800 000 0139** and are never sent to chat or the app first.
4. The care assistant does not handle these contacts: it hands over at once (see `assistant-guardrails.md`).

## What we record

Every missed 2-hour callback is reported to the Head of Customer Care the next working day, with the
contact id and how long the customer waited. A person decides the follow-up; nobody writes to these
customers without that decision.
