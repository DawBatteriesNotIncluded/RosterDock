# Compensation policy (v7, in force since 1 April 2026)

Fictional, for the Cirrus demo. This replaces v6; anything that quotes v6 amounts is out of date.

## Automatic credit

| What went wrong                                                    | Automatic credit                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| Broadband not working for more than 2 full working days            | £9.80 for each further full day |
| An engineer visit missed, or cancelled with under 24 hours' notice | £30.50 for each missed visit    |
| A new service starting later than the date we promised             | £6.10 for each day of delay     |

- A loss of service of **less than 2 full working days earns no automatic credit**, however many customers
  it affected.
- Credits appear on the next bill. Nobody needs to claim an automatic credit.

## Goodwill

- For a fault that earns no automatic credit, an agent may offer a goodwill credit of **up to £5**, and a
  team leader **up to £20**, after looking at the account. It is decided case by case.
- **Nobody, and no automated system, promises a goodwill amount in advance or in a message sent to more
  than one customer.** A message may say that customers can contact us and that we will look at each case.
- A pack (Sport, Cinema, Kids) is credited pro rata only when the pack itself was unavailable for more than
  24 hours.

## Billing errors

- Money taken in error is refunded in full to the original payment method within 5 working days of the
  error being confirmed, without the customer having to ask.

## What v6 said (withdrawn)

v6 gave a flat £25 automatic credit for any loss of service longer than 3 hours. It was withdrawn on
1 April 2026 and must not be quoted.
