# What to do when your broadband is down

_Help centre article HC-014. Last reviewed: 12 January 2026._

## First, check your Hub

- A **green** light means your Hub is working. Try restarting the device you are using.
- A **flashing orange** light means the Hub is starting up. Give it five minutes.
- A **red** light means no connection. Turn the Hub off at the wall, wait 30 seconds and turn it on again.

## Check for a fault in your area

Look at the service status page. If there is a fault where you live, our engineers are already working on
it and you do not need to contact us.

## Compensation

If your broadband is down for more than 3 hours you will automatically get a **£25 credit** on your next
bill. You do not need to do anything.

## Still not working?

Chat to us in the app. Our assistant can run a line test and book an engineer.
